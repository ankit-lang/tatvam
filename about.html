About Tatvam Consulting

Finance expertise connected to business operations

We help management teams strengthen reporting, controls and finance processes, and provide specialist support where internal teams require additional capacity or technical depth.

What the firm does

Tatvam Consulting provides management and finance advisory services across assurance support, accounting, internal controls, risk, managed finance operations, valuation and regulatory matters.

Assignments range from recurring bookkeeping and MIS support to audit readiness, financial reporting projects, control remediation and finance transformation.

How we work

  • Engagement scope tied to a clear reporting or business requirement
  • Senior review at important stages of the assignment
  • Working papers and conclusions documented for management review
  • Coordination with auditors, advisors and internal stakeholders
  • Implementation support where the assignment requires process change
